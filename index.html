A proofing sheet for Unicode blocks. Drop a font to see which blocks it covers, which characters are still missing, and what it maps beyond the standard. Everything runs in your browser — the file never leaves your machine.

Drop a font anywhere on this page, or browse — OTF · TTF · WOFF · WOFF2
